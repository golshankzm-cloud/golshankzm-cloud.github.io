How I work

A logo is a start. A system is what people use.

For each project below, I went back to my original files and extended the identity into a working system. Every page says what is original and what is new.

01 · Find the idea

Start from the story: who the brand is for and what it wants people to feel.

02 · Make one rule

Turn the idea into one rule that builds everything: a module, a shape, a line.

03 · Make it measurable

Colour scales, semantic tokens, light and dark modes, and WCAG contrast checks.

04 · Prove it

Test it on real touchpoints: web, UI components, packaging and social.
